fade's 6th release, and their 4th EP so far. Produced by Hajime Okano, the track Born Ready features Koie Kenta of Crossfaith, a cover of Frankie Goes To Hollywood's *Relax* (on the CD version) and a cover of Bon Jovi's *Livin' On A Prayer* (on the iTunes version). Additionally, a European edition was released through Gan-Shin Records which contains both covers, as well as an acoustic version of *Face* from their last album, *Age of Innocence*.

A music video for コズミカリズム exists; you can watch it [here](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wpj0YlKjctM).
